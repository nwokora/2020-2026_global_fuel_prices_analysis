Findings section
1. Dataset coverage
The analysis covered 84 countries across 7 continental regions.

2. Regional fuel price distribution
Europe has the highest average fuel price at 3.33.

Oceania follows closely at 3.29.

The Middle East has the lowest average fuel price at 1.10.

South America and Africa also record relatively low averages at 1.26 and 1.38.

3. Country-level fuel price extremes
Europe contains 8 of the top 10 countries with the highest average fuel prices.

The Middle East and Africa dominate the top 10 countries with the lowest average fuel prices.

4. Yearly fuel price trend
Average fuel prices increased from 2020 to 2026.

There was a small dip in 2023, after which prices rose again.

5. Regional tax burden
Oceania has the highest average tax percentage at about 39%.

Europe follows at about 35%.

South America and the Middle East have lower averages at 18% and 20%.

6. Highest taxpayers
Rwanda has the highest tax percentage at 39.6%.

Europe contains 7 of the top 10 highest-taxing countries.

Africa has 4 countries among the lowest taxpayers, followed by the Middle East with 2 countries.

7. Regional subsidy distribution
The Middle East has the highest average subsidy level at 3.00.

South America follows at 2.43.

Africa averages 2.00.

Europe and Oceania have the lowest averages at 1.17 and 1.00.

8. Subsidy level distribution by region
Oceania has 100% low subsidy.

Europe has 86.96% low subsidy.

The Middle East shows the strongest support, with 55.56% very high subsidies.

9. Low-income affordability under subsidy levels
For low-income countries, affordability improves as subsidy level increases.

Countries such as Venezuela, Libya, Egypt, and Nigeria are the most favoured under very high subsidies.

Uganda, Mozambique, Zambia, Rwanda, and Zimbabwe are the least favoured and more likely to face stronger affordability pressure.
